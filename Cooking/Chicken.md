# Chicken

## Pan
1. Put Oil, Jeera, Tejpatta, laal mirch, whole garam masala and heat it
2. Put onion and salt and roast it till the color of onion is translucent
3. Put chicken and ginger garlic paste, termuric, red chilly powder, chicken masala and jeera masala
4. roast it

## Cooker
1. Thin chopped onions
3. Wash chiken
4. mix onion with chicken
5. turmuric and jeera marich powder in it mixed
6. put mashed garlic (1.5 garlic per half kg) in it
7. put curd in it
8. salt in it
9. put garm masala in it and mix
10. put chicken masala and mix
11. mix it well and leave it for 30 minutes
12. in cooker, put oil jeera tejpatta laalmirch and hing and heat it
13. put marinated chicken in it
14. cook on sim flame
15. put little water (half cup per half kg)
16. 3 seeti on low flame
