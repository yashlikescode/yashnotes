# Chicken

## Pan
1. Put Oil, Jeera, Tejpatta, laal mirch, whole garam masala and heat it
2. Put onion and salt and roast it till the color of onion is translucent
3. Put chicken and ginger garlic paste, termuric, red chilly powder, chicken masala and jeera masala
4. roast it

## Cooker
1. Thin chopped onions
3. Wash chiken
4. mix onion with chicken
5. turmuric and jeera marich powder in it mixed
6. put mashed garlic (1.5 garlic per half kg) in it
7. put curd in it
8. salt in it
9. put garm masala in it and mix
10. put chicken masala and mix
11. mix it well and leave it for 30 minutes
12. in cooker, put oil jeera tejpatta laalmirch and hing and heat it
13. put marinated chicken in it
14. cook on sim flame
15. put little water (half cup per half kg)
16. 3 seeti on low flame

## Brar's Chicken Curry

- put chicken, salt, turmeric, red chilli powsder, ginger garlic paste
- mustard oil 1 tbsp and mix and marinate
- leave it from 15-20 minutes to 1 hr
- chop onions
- 2-3 tomato, put a little water and grind to make tomato puree
- thick chopped green chilli
- heat cooker and put mustard oil and a little bit of ghee
- put bayleaf, cumin seeds heat it just a bit and put onion, put ginger garlic paste and salt
- corriander leaf stem
- when onion is cooked till it has some brown edges, put chicken
- chicken will first leave water then leave oil
- when chicken leaves oil, put tomato puree
- cook till chicken again leaves oil
- put a little bit of oil
- let the water boil for a bit
- put garam masala and dhaniya masala
- let it boil for at least 10 minutes
- put thick corriender leaves
